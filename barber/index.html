Before You Book

These keep everyone’s time on schedule, yours and the next client’s.

  • Be on time

    If you’re 15 minutes or more late, there’s a $5 late fee, paid at the chair. If it isn’t paid, it’s added to your next cut.

  • Deposit holds your spot

    Send half the price by Cash App or Zelle right after you book. No deposit, no appointment. It comes off your total.

  • Can’t make it?

    Text me at least 24 hours before your time to cancel or move it. If you cancel later than that or don’t show up, the deposit is kept.

  • Booking for a kid?

    A parent or guardian books, and the kids price is for anyone 15 and under.

  • Want a certain style?

    Write what you want when you book and add a photo if you have one.

  • Where

    It’s a private home studio, not a shop. You get the address once your deposit comes in.

Questions? Text me. I’ll answer when I’m not cutting.

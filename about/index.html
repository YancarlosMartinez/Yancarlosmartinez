About Yancarlos

Yancarlos Martinez is a cinematographer, film editor, actor, 3D animator and VFX artist. He’s recognized on IMDb for editing the feature film The Families Feud, and he has shot, edited and acted in films, series, music videos and reels with collaborators like Ubong Inyang, Bruce “Busta” Soscia and Joe McGee.

He builds original 3D characters and universes, including Broccarlos, Avictor and Dot Robot, in Character Creator 5 and Blender, lit and rendered in Unreal Engine 5. He also develops games in Unreal Engine and Unity, and builds websites and interactive 3D pages.

He trained at the New York Film Academy (Class of 2019) and Goodwin College, and works as a commercial print model, including the cover of Fable Manny.

UI

Ubong Inyang

Director • Producer • Actor • Screenwriter

Known as UB, Ubong Inyang is an award-winning filmmaker who moved to the U.S. from Nigeria in 2011 and runs Ubong Inyang Production USA in Bloomfield, Connecticut. His film Sunita won Best Trailer and Best African American Actor in a Movie at the 2021 IndieFEST Film Awards; On IMDb he’s known for Backus, Sunita (2023) and Yes I Do (2024), and he wrote and produced Curse of the River (2025).

Together: UB has been my main collaborator in film for more than five years, across his YouTube channels, videos and feature films, and I’ve been part of most of his movies. I edited Curse of the River, now on Prime Video, and worked on Sunita, Costly Mistake and UB’s Secret. We just wrapped Backus, an action feature UB directed, co-wrote and stars in as Backus, and it’s now in post-production.

Bruce “Busta” Soscia

Actor • Producer • Entrepreneur

An actor and producer raised in Rhode Island, Bruce has appeared in Dog Gone (Netflix), Godmothered, Defending Jacob, The Blacklist and Gravesend, and plays a co-lead in Winterset.

Joe McGee

Writer • Director • Producer • Hawk Studios

Born in Hartford, Connecticut, Joe runs Hawk Studios, formerly McGee Productions USA, and is credited on IMDb as Joe Hawk. A former manager and technical writer, he wrote the book Leadership Lessons Inspired By A Six Year Old and the novel The White House Is Fake, and he wrote, directed and produced the sci-fi series Reflections: Project Chameleon.

Together: I filmed, edited and acted in Reflections: Project Chameleon, now on Prime Video, and was on set for his short film For Bobby.

JY

Joe Young

Cartoonist • Animator • Filmmaker

Born in Hartford, Joseph “Joe” Young Jr. has drawn the Scruples comic strip since 1989 and runs the Hartford Animation & Film Institute. In 1999 he drew a 100-yard Scruples strip that set the Guinness World Record for the world’s longest comic strip, and he received a Daily Point of Light Award from the White House. He also turned his graphic novel Diamond Ruff into his first feature film.

Together: I filmed interviews with Joe, and we shot a commercial together at his studio.

VC

VirtualCons

Fan Convention Company • Rhode Island

VirtualCons was a Rhode Island company, led by CEO and co-founder Michael Mota, whose app brought conventions to fans online: one-on-one celebrity meet-and-greets, personalized shoutouts, never-before-seen footage and year-round virtual events such as SopranosCon, MobMovieCon and BoxingCon. In July 2021 it brought MobMovieCon to Harrah’s Resort in Atlantic City, with Ice-T hosting the MobMovieAwards. Its circle included Armand Assante, Taral Hicks and Premium Pete.

Together: I worked with the VirtualCons team in Smithfield, Rhode Island, alongside editor and camera operator Dan Ryan. I ran camera at MobMovieCon in Atlantic City and edited videos like the cigar-burn intro with Armand Assante, Taral Hicks’s reel and The Premium Pete Show outro.

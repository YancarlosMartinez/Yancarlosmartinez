Featured project • 3D design • Charity

Ms. Trish Self-Care Salon

Patricia “Ms. Trish” Candillo spent her life caring for others. After weeks in the hospital herself, she imagined a self-care salon inside it for patients, parents and staff. Her family is now raising the funds to build it at Connecticut Children’s Medical Center. I built the salon in 3D, modeled every piece of furniture, and produced the narrated presentation for the CCMC Charity Luncheon.

Collaborators

The filmmakers, actors, hosts and companies I’ve made these projects with.

UI

Ubong Inyang

Director • Producer • Actor • Screenwriter

Known as UB, Ubong Inyang is an award-winning filmmaker who moved to the U.S. from Nigeria in 2011 and runs Ubong Inyang Production USA in Bloomfield, Connecticut. His film Sunita won Best Trailer and Best African American Actor in a Movie at the 2021 IndieFEST Film Awards; his other films include Yes I Do, Curse of the River and Soulmate.

Together: I’ve worked with UB for more than five years on his YouTube channels, videos and feature films, including Sunita, Costly Mistake and UB’s Secret, and I turned him into a 3D cartoon. We’re now making his next movie, Backus.

Website ↗IMDb ↗YouTube ↗
BS

Bruce “Busta” Soscia

Actor • Producer • Entrepreneur

An actor and producer raised in Rhode Island, Bruce has appeared in Dog Gone (Netflix), Godmothered, Defending Jacob, The Blacklist and Gravesend, and plays a co-lead in Winterset.

Together: Bruce has a supporting role in The Families Feud and is executive producer of The Tremont. I edited his actor reel and its intro.

IMDb ↗
JM

Joe McGee

Writer • Director • Producer

Born in Hartford, Connecticut, Joe founded McGee Productions USA, whose motto is “Turning Novels into Films.” A former manager and technical writer, he wrote the book Leadership Lessons Inspired By A Six Year Old and the novel The White House Is Fake, and he wrote, directed and produced the sci-fi series Reflections: Project Chameleon.

Together: I filmed, edited and acted in Reflections: Project Chameleon and was on set for his short film For Bobby. Both are on Amazon Prime.

Reflections ↗Interview ↗
JY

Joe Young

Cartoonist • Animator • Filmmaker

Born in Hartford, Joseph “Joe” Young Jr. has drawn the Scruples comic strip since 1989 and runs the Hartford Animation & Film Institute. In 1999 he drew a 100-yard Scruples strip that set the Guinness World Record for the world’s longest comic strip, and he received a Daily Point of Light Award from the White House. He also turned his graphic novel Diamond Ruff into his first feature film.

Together: I set up the camera, sound and lighting for a shoot at his studio.

Wikipedia ↗Interview ↗
VC

VirtualCons

Fan Convention Company • Rhode Island

VirtualCons was a Rhode Island company, led by CEO and co-founder Michael Mota, whose app brought conventions to fans online: one-on-one celebrity meet-and-greets, personalized shoutouts, never-before-seen footage and year-round virtual events such as SopranosCon, MobMovieCon and BoxingCon. In July 2021 it brought MobMovieCon to Harrah’s Resort in Atlantic City, with Ice-T hosting the MobMovieAwards. Its circle included Armand Assante, Taral Hicks and Premium Pete.

Together: I worked with the VirtualCons team in Smithfield, Rhode Island, alongside editor and camera operator Dan Ryan. I ran camera at MobMovieCon in Atlantic City and edited videos like the cigar-burn intro with Armand Assante, Taral Hicks’s reel and The Premium Pete Show outro.

Press release ↗MobMovieCon ↗
AA

Armand Assante

Actor • Emmy Winner

An actor from New York City, Armand Assante won a Primetime Emmy for playing John Gotti in HBO’s Gotti (1996) and is known for The Odyssey and I, the Jury. In 2021 he became chairman of the board of VirtualCons and joined the Gotti screening and panel at MobMovieCon in Atlantic City.

Together: He’s featured in the VirtualCons title intro I edited.

Wikipedia ↗
IT

Ice-T

Rapper • Actor • Body Count Frontman

Born Tracy Marrow in Newark, New Jersey, Ice-T plays Odafin “Fin” Tutuola on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit and fronts the metal band Body Count. He has won two Grammys, for “Back on the Block” (1991) and Body Count’s “Bum-Rush” (2021), and got his star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in 2023.

Together: Ice-T hosted the MobMovieAwards at VirtualCons’ MobMovieCon at Harrah’s in Atlantic City, where I worked camera.

Walk of Fame ↗MobMovieCon ↗
TH

Taral Hicks

Actress • R&B Singer

Born in the Bronx, Taral Hicks is known for A Bronx Tale (1993), Belly (1998) and The Preacher’s Wife. She signed with Motown in 1995 and released her album This Time in 1997. She was part of VirtualCons, from its 2021 launch to MobMovieCon in Atlantic City.

Together: I edited her video reel at VirtualCons.

IMDb ↗Wikipedia ↗
PP

Premium Pete

Podcast Host • Producer • Entrepreneur

Premium Pete hosts The Premium Pete Show, a podcast about food, hip-hop, sneakers and entertainment. A former co-host of The Combat Jack Show, he has produced podcasts for State Farm and HBO and co-founded Grandeza Hot Sauce and Pete’s Premium Pasta Sauce. He was part of VirtualCons and a guest at MobMovieCon.

Together: I edited the square outro for The Premium Pete Show at VirtualCons.

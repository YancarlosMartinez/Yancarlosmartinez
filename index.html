Featured project • 3D design • Charity

Ms. Trish Self-Care Salon

Patricia “Ms. Trish” Candillo spent her life caring for others. After weeks in the hospital herself, she imagined a self-care salon inside it for patients, parents and staff. Her family is now raising the funds to build it at Connecticut Children’s Medical Center. I built the salon in 3D, modeled every piece of furniture, and produced the narrated presentation for the CCMC Charity Luncheon.

How It Works

Simple and upfront. You know the price before I start.

  1. Tell me what you need

    Send the clip length, your deadline and any examples you like. I send a free quote, usually within a day or two.

  2. New clients: 50% off

    Your first project with me is half price, up to $250 off. A $100 cleanup shot is $50, and a $200 clone shot is $100.

  3. Half to start

    50% up front to book your spot, and the rest when you approve the final version. Apps and games are paid in stages.

  4. Your deadline sets the price

    Need it in less than 3 days? Rush jobs add 25%. More time means the regular price.

  5. Two rounds of changes

    Two rounds of changes are included. More changes after that are $50 an hour.

  6. Come back and save

    Returning clients get 25% off their next project, up to $250 off. Booking filming, editing and effects together costs less than booking them one by one.
